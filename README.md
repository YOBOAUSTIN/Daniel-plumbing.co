# Daniel Plumbing Co.

Responsive website for Daniel A. Kamara, serving plumbing enquiries for New England, Freetown, Sierra Leone. Open index.html or use a static web server.

## Before publishing

- Add the final domain and submit it to Google Search Console after hosting. Update the JSON-LD business URL when a permanent domain is available.
- Claim and verify the Google Business Profile for the real business location/service area. The embedded map points to New England, Freetown; confirm the exact public business address before publishing it.
- Configure Decap CMS at /admin by setting your GitHub repository in admin/config.yml and enabling authentication. The CMS can update service area and contact/social/payment details.
- Add an email address only if Daniel wants one published. Contact requests currently open a WhatsApp message to +232 79 351 163.
- Confirm Orange Money recipient, pricing and payment terms directly with Daniel before sending money. The site does not collect or process payment credentials.
- Replace the sample project photos and any sample copy with Daniel's verified project images, experience, qualifications, certifications and approved client reviews.
- Product cards are enquiry placeholders. Secure online checkout needs a merchant account and server-side payment integration.

Google ranking cannot be guaranteed. Accurate business information, useful localized content and a verified Business Profile can help Google understand the service.
